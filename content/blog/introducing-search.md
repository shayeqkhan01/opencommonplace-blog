Today, we’re introducing **Search** on Open Commonplace.

As your commonplace grows, remembering *that you wrote something* is easy. Remembering **where** you wrote it is not.

Search is built to make that problem disappear.

Type a word, phrase, book, author, or idea, and instantly find the notes that contain it. Whether you’re looking for something you saved yesterday or something you wrote months ago, Search gives you a direct path back to it.

## Your commonplace, at your fingertips

Open Commonplace is meant to grow with you. The more you read, the more ideas you collect, and the more useful your personal knowledge base becomes.

But a large collection is only valuable if you can retrieve what you need.

Search makes your notes **discoverable**.

Looking for that idea you saved about incentives? Search it.

Trying to find a note from a book you read last year? Search it.

Remember only a fragment of a thought? Search for the words you remember.

You don't need to remember where an idea lives. **You just need to remember what you're looking for.**

## Built for a growing commonplace

Search is only the beginning.

As Open Commonplace grows, we're building toward a system where your accumulated knowledge becomes easier to navigate, connect, and rediscover.

Because a commonplace isn't just a place to store things.

**It's a place to return to them.**

Search is now available on Open Commonplace.
