About

August 17, 2026 · Open Commonplace

Open Commonplace is a shared digital commonplace book.

We collect and share book notes, key takeaways, mental models, and memorable passages — so insights from great books stay useful and discoverable.

Anyone can contribute. The goal is simple: make reading more cumulative and less ephemeral.
