How to make a commonplace book useful

A simple starting point for turning scattered highlights into ideas you can return to.
August 19, 2026 · Open Commonplace

The best notes are not the ones you collect. They are the ones you can find again when a good question arrives.

This is the way we are trying to do things here: capture the idea, add enough context to make it useful, and leave a trail for the next reader.
